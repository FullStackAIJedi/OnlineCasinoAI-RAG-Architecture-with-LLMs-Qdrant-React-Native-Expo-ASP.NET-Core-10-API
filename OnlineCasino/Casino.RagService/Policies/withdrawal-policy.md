# Withdrawal Policy

## Category
withdrawal

## Summary
This policy covers withdrawal methods, processing times, and limits.

## Rules
- Minimum withdrawal is $20.
- Withdrawals require completed KYC verification.
- E-wallet withdrawals are processed within 24 hours.
- Bank transfers may take 1-5 business days.

## Limits
- Minimum withdrawal: $20
- Maximum withdrawal per transaction: $5,000
- Daily withdrawal limit: $10,000

## Examples
- Valid: A verified player withdraws $100 via e-wallet and receives funds within 24 hours.
- Invalid: An unverified player attempts to withdraw without completing KYC.

## Actions
- Complete KYC via the profile page before requesting a withdrawal.
- Contact support@casino.com for withdrawal issues.
