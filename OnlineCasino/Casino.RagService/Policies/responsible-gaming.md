# Responsible Gaming Policy

## Category
responsible-gaming

## Summary
This policy describes tools and limits that help players gamble responsibly.

## Rules
- Players can set daily, weekly, or monthly deposit limits.
- Self-exclusion is available for 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or permanently.
- All limits take effect immediately and cannot be increased for 24 hours.

## Limits
- Minimum deposit limit: $10 per day
- Maximum self-exclusion period: permanent

## Examples
- Valid: A player sets a $50 daily deposit limit and is blocked after reaching it.
- Invalid: A player requests to increase their limit immediately after setting it.

## Actions
- Contact support@casino.com for responsible gaming assistance.
